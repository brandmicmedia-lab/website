Design a bold, modern Gen-Z style digital marketing agency website in Figma for a brand named “Brandmic media”.

Inspiration:
Clean structured agency layout with hero, stats, about, services, CTA banner, team, newsletter and footer. Minimal but visually engaging.

Brand Colors:
Primary: #FF0091 (vibrant pink)
Background: #F9F9F9 (soft white)
Dark Sections: #000000 (black)

Typography:
Bold modern sans-serif (Inter, Satoshi, or Space Grotesk style)
Large expressive hero typography (64–72px)
Strong contrast hierarchy
Use lowercase + bold styling for Gen-Z feel

Style Direction:
- Gen-Z modern startup vibe
- Clean but expressive
- Large typography
- Bold spacing
- Floating 3D abstract elements (pink glossy blobs / gradient spheres)
- Soft glow effects
- Rounded cards (20–24px radius)
- Glassmorphism subtle cards
- Smooth layered depth

Layout (1440px desktop, 12-column grid)
